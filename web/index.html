    About this model

    A gradient-boosted model (LightGBM) trained on about 20,000 US postings that disclosed a salary, published April to September 2026. Text is hashed into bag-of-words features, so no vocabulary is shipped; employer identity is a target-encoded lookup. The five blocks were randomly dropped during training so the model behaves sensibly when you switch them off.

    Errors quoted above are measured on the newest 15% of postings, which the model never saw. The estimate is what a disclosing employer would advertise, not what a specific person would be offered. Individual-contributor roles only, mostly data, analytics and software.
